# Order Management

## 1. Purpose
This BRD describes order capture and fulfilment changes for the CRM and ERP programme of a medical products distributor.

## Order capture
BR-101: The system shall allow a sales representative to create orders for hospital and pharmacy customers from the account page.
BR-102: The system shall validate customer licence status before an order for controlled substances is submitted.
BR-103: The system must display contract pricing from the customer's GPO agreement on each order line.
BR-104: Customer service agents should be able to place orders on behalf of a clinic and the order screens must be user-friendly.

## Cold chain fulfilment
BR-201: The system shall reserve refrigerated vaccine inventory only from distribution centers with validated 2-8 C storage.
BR-202: Warehouse operators must record temperature readings at pick and pack for biologic products.
BR-203: The system must respond to inventory availability checks within 2 seconds for 95% of requests.

## Returns and recalls
BR-301: The system shall allow customers to request returns of non-expired med-surg supplies within 30 days.
BR-302: Compliance must be able to identify and quarantine affected lots within 4 hours of a recall notice.
BR-303: Returned implants and devices require UDI capture before credit is issued, rules TBD.

## Billing
BR-401: Finance shall process wholesaler chargeback claims and reconcile them against contract pricing.
BR-402: All pricing changes must be captured in an audit trail with user, timestamp and reason.
